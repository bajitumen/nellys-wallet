How to set up Plaid

Nelly's Wallet reads your account balances and transactions through Plaid. Plaid is free for personal use — you just need to make an account and grab two keys.

  1. Create a Plaid account

    Sign up at dashboard.plaid.com/signup. Use a personal email — no credit card required.

  2. Tell Plaid what you're building

    When asked about your use case, choose Hobbyist and Personal finances. This routes you to the free Developer plan instead of an enterprise sales call.

  3. Sign up for the free Developer tier

    The free tier gives you 10 live items (10 linked institutions) — plenty for personal use. No payment info needed.

  4. Copy your credentials

    In the Plaid dashboard:

    1. Open the Developers tab in the left sidebar.
    2. Click Keys.
    3. Copy your client_id and your Production secret.

  5. Paste them in

    Head back to the credentials page, paste both values, and save. From there, click the + button in the header to link your first account.

Your credentials are stored Fernet-encrypted at rest. Plaid charges Nelly's Wallet nothing — you pay nothing.
